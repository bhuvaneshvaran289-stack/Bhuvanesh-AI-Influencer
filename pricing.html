FAQ

Pricing & trial — frequently asked.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, any time — up or down. Plenrik prorates the difference automatically, so there's no awkward call to billing.

What counts as a "campaign"?

One creator, one brief, one paid placement set. You can pause and resume the same campaign across months without it counting twice.

Do you offer non-profit pricing?

Yes — 50% off on Starter and Scale for registered non-profits. Email the team and we'll set you up.

How does the free trial work?

14 days, full platform unlocked, no credit card required. After the trial, you can downgrade to a paid plan or your account simply pauses — your data is preserved for 90 days.

What's the payment fee for creators?

1.5% of the creator payout, capped at $150 per campaign. Covers FX, tax-form handling, and milestone escrow.

Where is my data stored?

Primary data centre is AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland). Enterprise plan customers can opt-in to a US-only or APAC-only deployment.
